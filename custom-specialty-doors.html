What's Involved

A door is a system, too, just one that's easy to leave for last.

Custom and specialty doors, the front entry that sets the tone for a home, a pivot door into a study, a pocket door that disappears into a wall, often get treated as an afterthought, chosen late and rushed into the build. Because they carry longer lead times than stock options, that's exactly backwards.

We treat doors and specialty materials the same way we treat AV systems: specified early, coordinated with the build sequence, and sourced from the right suppliers for the design intent and budget. That comes from Hodge Industries' background in construction, not just technology consulting, and it's why we're just as comfortable sourcing a door as we are specifying the system behind the wall next to it.

Installation is handled by your builder or a licensed installer; our role is getting the right door, material, and hardware specified and ordered in time.

  • Front entry doorsThe first impression of the home, sourced to match the architecture.
  • Interior statement piecesPivot, pocket, barn, and other specialty doors for rooms that call for something more than a standard slab.
  • Materials & finishesSourced to match the design language of the build, not chosen from a generic catalog.
  • Hardware coordinationHandles, hinges, and pulls specified alongside the door itself.
  • Schedule coordinationOrdered early enough to account for longer custom lead times.

Common Questions

What people usually ask about custom doors and materials.

Do you install the doors yourselves?+

We source and specify: choosing the right door, material, and hardware for the design intent and coordinating delivery with your build schedule. Installation is carried out by your builder or a licensed installer, with us involved to keep it aligned with the design.

Can you source doors without the AV or smart home scope?+

Yes. Doors and specialty materials sourcing stands on its own and doesn't require also engaging our AV and automation services.

What styles and materials do you work with?+

From front entry doors to interior statement pieces like pivot, pocket, and barn doors, in a range of materials and finishes matched to the architecture and budget of the project.

How far in advance do custom doors need to be ordered?+

Custom and specialty doors often carry longer lead times than stock options, so we like to be involved early enough to place orders well ahead of the point they're needed on site.
