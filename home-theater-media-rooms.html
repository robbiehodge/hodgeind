What's Involved

Every theater or media room decision has a right order.

Screen size drives seating distance. Seating distance drives room depth. Room depth drives where the equipment rack and wiring need to land. Get the order wrong and you end up compromising on all of it after the fact.

We work through that sequence with you early, whether you're planning a dedicated theater with full acoustic treatment and light control, or a more flexible media room that still needs to work as a living space. For homes that are already finished, we find wireless and minimally invasive ways to deliver the same result without unnecessary demolition.

Every plan is coordinated with your architect and licensed electrician, so conduit, power, and structural needs exist before they're needed, not after drywall.

  • Room layout & sightlinesSeating position, screen size, and viewing angles specified together, not guessed at separately.
  • AcousticsSound isolation and in-room acoustic treatment appropriate to a dedicated theater or a shared media space.
  • Screen & projector or TV specificationEquipment chosen for the room's light control and use case, not a generic recommendation.
  • SeatingRiser and seating plans that keep sightlines clean at every row.
  • Control integrationOne simple control experience for lighting, shading, and playback, tied into your broader smart home system if you have one.

Common Questions

What people usually ask about home theaters and media rooms.

Do I need a dedicated room, or can this work in a bonus room or basement?+

Either. A dedicated, light-controlled theater and an everyday media room use the same design fundamentals, just tuned differently. We specify the layout, acoustics, and equipment to match the room you actually have.

What's the difference between a media room and a home theater?+

A home theater is typically a dedicated, purpose-built space with controlled lighting and acoustic treatment for a true cinema experience. A media room is a more flexible everyday space, often doubling as a living or bonus room, tuned for good sound and picture without giving up the room's other uses.

How much does a home theater setup cost?+

It depends heavily on room size, screen or projector choice, and how much acoustic treatment and seating are involved. Share your project details through our contact form and we'll follow up with a straightforward estimate.

Can this be added to a home that's already built?+

Yes. We regularly design theaters and media rooms as retrofits, using wireless and minimally invasive approaches where running new wiring isn't practical.
