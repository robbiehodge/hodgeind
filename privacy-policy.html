Privacy Policy

Last updated August 12, 2026

Hodge Industries ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a project inquiry or quick-contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and any project details you share. This site does not use cookies or tracking scripts to collect personal information.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and follow up about your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are processed and stored securely by Netlify, our website hosting provider, in accordance with Netlify's own privacy practices.

Questions
