Our Story

Built from firsthand frustration

We started Hodge Industries after facing long lead times and high costs while trying to source accordion glass doors for our own home project. What began as a search for a better solution turned into a business built around quality, customization, and speed.

Based in North Carolina, we design and install premium aluminum and glass folding door systems that connect indoor and outdoor spaces with style and function — all backed by reliable service and a strong warranty.
