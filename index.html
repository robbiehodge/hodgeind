Why Bring In a Consultant

The cost of AV is decided in week two, not week twenty.

Most AV problems on a build aren't technology problems. They're sequencing problems. By the time a system is chosen after drywall, options are limited and costs go up. Bringing in AV consulting alongside your architect means the right conduit, power, and structure exist before they're needed. For homes that are already finished, we bring that same systems thinking to work within existing walls instead.

01

About

A construction company, not just an AV shop.

Hodge Industries has built its name on architect-led custom builds and renovations, and AV and smart home consulting is where most of that work lives today. But almost every project has involved more than wiring and equipment, from custom doors to other specialty materials we've sourced and installed alongside the technology systems. That hands-on view of the full build, not just the AV scope, is what gives us a builder's-eye read on where technology planning typically goes wrong, and how early, coordinated consulting fixes it.

Today that experience shapes how we work with builders, architects, and homeowners: practical, coordinated with the rest of the trades, and just as comfortable sourcing a door or material for the project as we are specifying the systems behind the walls. Both are built to hold up long after the final walkthrough.

Common Questions

What people usually ask before reaching out.

Do I need to hire an AV consultant before choosing a builder?+

It helps to bring us in as early as possible, ideally alongside your architect, but we regularly join projects that are already under construction or fully built. Earlier just means more options and less rework.

Do you sell or install the equipment yourselves?+

We consult and specify: designing the system, choosing equipment, and coordinating the plan. Installation is carried out by your licensed electrician and integrators, with us involved to keep it aligned with the design.

What if construction has already started, or the home is finished?+

That's exactly what our retrofit and existing-home upgrade work covers. We find wireless and minimally invasive ways to deliver the same systems without opening up walls unnecessarily.

How much does AV consulting typically cost?+

It depends on the size of the home and how many systems are involved. Share your project details through the form below and we'll follow up with a straightforward estimate.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in Charlotte, NC, and take on projects throughout the surrounding region.
